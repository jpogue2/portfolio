I **designed and fabricated** an interactive set piece for an **upcoming escape room**. The prop integrates embedded sensors with a [COGS](https://cogs.show/) backend and contains several features to ensure robustness and maintainability. 

The most challenging aspect of the project was balancing **aesthetics, functionality, and cost**. Rather than relying on fully custom components, I iterated through numerous design concepts and off-the-shelf materials to create a prop that felt unique while remaining practical to build and maintain.
